Amy Placeholder is a painter and printmaker based in London. Her work explores coastlines, weather and the slow change of familiar places, moving between oil painting, drawing and traditional print processes.

She studied Fine Art at a placeholder art school and has exhibited across the UK.

## Selected exhibitions

- **2025** — *Tidal*, solo exhibition, Placeholder Gallery, London
- **2024** — Summer Open, Example Arts Centre, Bristol
- **2023** — *Ground*, group exhibition, Sample Projects, Margate

## Education

- **2019** — MA Fine Art, Placeholder School of Art
- **2016** — BA (Hons) Painting, Example University
